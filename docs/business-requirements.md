# Bargain Bank Business Requirements

## Executive Summary
Bargain Bank is a simple, gamified savings app designed to motivate everyday people to develop better financial habits. By leveraging dopamine-driven feedback and playful design, Bargain Bank transforms saving money from a chore into a rewarding, habit-forming experience.

## Product Vision & Motto
- **Vision:** Make saving money fun, easy, and habit-forming for everyone.
- **Motto:** "Simplicity is best."

## Target Users (Persona)
- Everyday individuals seeking to improve their savings habits
- Not finance experts; prefer simple, intuitive tools
- Likely to be demotivated by traditional, data-heavy financial apps
- Value instant feedback and positive reinforcement

## Problem & Solution Statement
- **Problem:** Most people struggle to save consistently because traditional financial apps are boring, complex, and lack immediate rewards.
- **Solution:** Bargain Bank uses gamification and instant positive feedback to make saving money feel rewarding, fun, and easy to maintain as a daily habit.

## MVP Scope
### In Scope
- Simple onboarding
- Create and track savings goals
- Gamified savings actions (streaks, badges, progress bars)
- Instant positive feedback (animations, sounds, messages)
- Visual progress tracking
- Daily/weekly challenges
- Motivational reminders

### Out of Scope
- Investment products
- Social/sharing features
- Bank account integrations
- Advanced analytics or financial planning

### Constraints
- Must be extremely simple and intuitive
- No financial jargon
- Mobile-first design

## High-Level Feature List
- User registration and onboarding
- Create/manage savings goals
- Record savings actions
- Gamification: badges, streaks, levels
- Visual feedback: animations, sounds, messages
- Progress tracking dashboard
- Reminders and motivational nudges
- Settings (basic preferences)

## Technical Constraints
- Mobile-first (responsive web or native app)
- Minimal data storage (user goals, actions, progress)
- No third-party financial integrations in MVP

## Open Questions
- What platforms to launch on first (iOS, Android, web)?
- Should there be a minimum savings amount?
- How to handle user authentication (email, phone, social)?
- What level of customization for feedback/animations?

## Appendix: Q&A Summary
1. **Core Purpose:** Motivate people to save more and develop financial habits
2. **Target Users:** Normal, simple people
3. **Value Proposition:** Dopamine loop, gamified savings
4. **Mechanics:** Rewarding feeling when recording savings
5. **MVP Scope:** Simple onboarding, savings goals, gamification, feedback, progress tracking, challenges, reminders
6. **Differentiation:** Playful, positive, micro-rewards, habit-forming
7. **Success Metrics:** Active users, savings actions, streaks, goals completed, retention
